What we recommend

  1. Move month-to-month customers onto 1–2 year contracts; they churn at 46% vs 3% on two-year plans.
  2. Escalate any customer on their 3rd support call; 88–100% of them leave.
  3. Fix plan mismatch: sell the international plan to active callers and push group plans, which churn at 7%.

Source: Databel customer dataset, n = 6,687 customers. Revenue at risk = sum of Monthly Charge for churned customers.
